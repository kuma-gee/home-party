# Castle Defense

Defend the castle using bow and arrow against the hoard of skeletons marching toward the castle

## Gameplay

The VR player uses bow and arrow to shoot down the enemies coming towards the castle. He can
choose up to 3 elements for the arrows to use, each having different cooldowns and effects.
Defend the castle for a specific amount of time without the gate being destroyed.

VR Player needs to physically grab the arrows from the back and select an element by putting
it into the right "magical orb" that changes the element. Shooting the bow by physically
grabbing the string and pulling.

The mobile playersa can spawn as skeletons and either move toward one of the two catapult to
charge a shot or pick up a bomb and run towards the gate. Reaching the gate with a bomb
increases the players fire power for the bombs and catapults. Multiple players can stay at
a catapult to increase the charging speed and power

- Time Limit: 5min
- Gate Health: 50
- Recommended player amount: 4+

**VR Elements**

- Fire(1s) - Creates an explosion on hit
- Ice(2s) - Freezes enemies in a range on hit and slows enemies moving inside the area
- Lightning(4s) - Lightning strikes in an area

**Mobile**

Every weapon has a base damage that is multiplied by the players fire power

- Left/Right Catapult
    - Base damage 3
    - 4s charge time
- 3x Bombs in the middle that can be picked up
    - Base damage 1
    - Explodes after 8 seconds after pickup
    - Increase firepower on reaching gate and exploding
    - Faster respawn
- Respawn time: 3 - 6s based on number of players
- Abilities: Dash (3s) or Shield (5s)
