# Castle Defense

Defend the castle using bow and arrow against the hoard of skeletons marching toward the castle

## Gameplay

The VR player uses bow and arrow to shoot down the enemies coming towards the castle. He can
choose up to 3 elements for the arrows to use, each having different cooldowns and effects.
Defend the castle for a specific amount of time without the gate being destroyed.

The mobile playersa can spawn as skeletons and either move toward one of the two catapult to
charge a shot or pick up a bomb and run towards the gate. Reaching the gate with a bomb
increases the players fire power for the bombs and catapults. Multiple players can stay at
a catapult to increase the charging speed and power

- Time Limit: 5min
- Gate Health: 50
- Recommended player amount: 4+

**VR Elements**

- Fire(0.5s) - Creates an explosion on hit
- Ice(3s) - Freezes enemies in a range on hit and slows enemies moving inside the area
- Earth(4s) - Spawn spikes on hit that also blocks the area for the enemies

**Mobile**

Every weapon has a base damage of 1 multiplied by the players fire power

- Left/Right Catapult - 5s charge time
- 3x Bombs - increase firepower on reaching gate and exploding
